THE SHORT VERSION

How to play

  1. 01
    Choose and draw

    Pick a mode, draw within 20L of ink, or jump in with cubes.

  2. 02
    Projectile Duel

    Take turns on one screen. Select a teammate, preview an equation, choose Force, then fire. Shots pierce enemies; stone walls block them.

  3. 03
    Bump Duel

    Copy the invite link into a second tab or device. Lock secret equations, launch together, and win by contact knockback. No bullets or projectile damage.
